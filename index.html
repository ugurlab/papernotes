PaperNotes

Save research articles, notes and quotations as you write.

Open PaperNotesDownload ZIP

What it does

Organise articles into Introduction, Methods, Results and Discussion. Search your archive by title, author, note or quotation. Export and import JSON backups.

How to use

  1. Add an article using its DOI.
  2. Look up article details or enter them manually.
  3. Choose a section, then save your notes and quotations.

Questions

Where are my records stored?

Your records stay in the browser you use. DOI lookup requires internet access and sends the DOI to public metadata services. To move an archive between devices or from the downloaded app to the web app, export and import a JSON backup.
